[Scala Days 2026](https://scaladays.org/) is just around the corner! Next week, on **12 and 13 October**, the Scala community will come together at Kulturbrauerei in Berlin for two days of learning, sharing experiences, and catching up with friends and colleagues.

The [program](https://scaladays.org/schedule/) opens with a keynote by Martin Odersky. There will also be interactive labs, giving you time to explore tools and ideas with their maintainers. Whether you use Scala every day or are getting to know the ecosystem, we look forward to welcoming you.

If you'd like to stay a little longer, [two-day workshops](https://scaladays.org/workshops/) follow on **14 and 15 October** at Hotel Oderberger, with practical sessions on AI-assisted development and Safe Scala. Workshops require a separate ticket or a conference and workshop bundle.

There are also [satellite events](https://scaladays.org/satellite-events/) around the conference: a [guided tech walk and dinner hosted by VirtusLab](https://virtuslab.com/lp/vip-tech-walk-through-berlin) on **11 October**, and [Typelevel Traverses Berlin](https://luma.com/iyv37emv) (waitlist) on **14 October**, hosted at Zalando. Both offer more time to meet fellow Scala developers and exchange ideas.

**[Buy your Scala Days ticket](https://tickets.plainschwarz.com/scaladays26/)** and join us in Berlin.

## Join us online, or meet your local community

If you can't travel to Berlin, you can still follow the conference from home. **[Buy a streaming ticket](https://tickets.plainschwarz.com/scaladays26/)** by choosing **Livestream Access** in the ticket shop. Tickets cost **€15** and provide access to the live conference sessions. Workshops and interactive labs are not included in the stream. You can find more details in the [online attendance announcement](https://scaladays.org/attend-scaladays-online/).

There are also plenty of opportunities to meet fellow Scala developers closer to home this October. Local meetups and conferences are a lovely way to exchange ideas, learn from each other, and get to know the people behind the projects you use.

## Scala events this October

Here are some upcoming events around the Scala and functional programming communities, in date order:

- **7 October, London, UK:** [London Scala Talks](https://www.meetup.com/london-scala/events/316551143/), with talks on Scala 3 specialization, probability and Balatro, and suspendable query DAGs.
- **8 October, Prague, Czechia:** [Functional JVM Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/functional-jvm-meetup/events/316648820/), with Arman Bilge on Scala Native and optimizing Cats Effect.
- **14 October, Stockholm, Sweden:** [Func Prog Conf](https://funcprogconf.com/), a day of functional programming talks and discussion, including Adam Warski on Scala 3 features for agentic development.
- **14 October, Berlin, Germany:** [Typelevel Traverses Berlin](https://luma.com/iyv37emv) (waitlist), with talks, a workshop, and an unconference after Scala Days.
- **15 October, Dublin, Ireland:** [Scala(3) Meetup: Rebooted](https://www.meetup.com/dublin-scala-users-group/events/314874170/), bringing the Dublin Scala community together again.
- **19 October, San Francisco, USA:** [Scala Summit](https://2026.scalasummit.com/), a day for the Bay Area Scala community.
- **20 October, Vienna, Austria:** [Scala Vienna User Group](https://www.meetup.com/scala-vienna/events/315074620/), with talks introducing Scala through the Typelevel ecosystem and exploring the Leucine actor framework.
- **22 October, Copenhagen, Denmark:** [Typelevel Traverses Copenhagen](https://luma.com/ncpbarok), with a workshop and evening talks.
- **24 October, London, UK:** [Typelevel Open Source Hack](https://luma.com/vzp35rp7) (waitlist), a day of working together on open source projects.
- **28 October, Málaga, Spain:** [Typelevel Traverses Málaga](https://luma.com/4s6qxb9p), with talks, a workshop, and an unconference ahead of Lambda World.
- **29 and 30 October, Torremolinos, Spain:** [Lambda World](https://www.lambda.world/), bringing together the wider functional programming community in the Málaga area.

Please check each event's page for registration and the latest practical details.

We look forward to seeing you in Berlin, online, or at a community event this October!
